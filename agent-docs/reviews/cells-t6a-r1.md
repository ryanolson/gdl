# The typed cell watch: review round 1

Reviewer: an independent reviewer, read-only, with the `thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review` and `rust-code-review` skills, in `/tmp` copies with their own target directories (2026-10-23). Target: `d0c0c8e..bef5bed`: the cell hub's test fixtures shared among test targets (`f2b2a0e`); `CellWatchEvent` and the `CellWatcher` client (`abd531a`); and the cell's hub serving `GET /v1/ext/gdl-cell/watch`, its feed of entry instances with `PeerInfo` from `GraphAdmission`, a shared bounded broadcast per cell, and `CellReadiness::watch()` in process (`bef5bed`). The reviewer ran fmt, clippy, the 46 `gdl-hub` tests, and the workspace suite. Verdict: REJECT, two major findings.

| # | Severity | Finding | Ruling | Evidence | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | A retirement seen on the loopback before the instance's `PeerInfo` was known was dropped, while its readiness was kept, so the instance was later sent as `Ready` | valid: velo-hub's service directory starts first, so a registration's services are broadcast before admission's commit hook gives the peer | `a_retirement_seen_before_the_peer_is_known_is_kept`: `[Ready]` where `[Retired]` was due | an instance the members do not hold yet keeps both flags, applied retirement first when it joins (`5d3ff4d`) |
| 2 | major | A departure emitted `Left` at once and erased a retirement still queued on the loopback, so `Left` came without `Retired` | valid for the reported interleaving: velo-hub calls extensions' removal hooks before the service directory drops the instance's services | `a_departure_waits_for_what_the_loopback_holds_before_it`: `[Ready, Left]` where `[Ready, Retired, Left]` was due | `left` waits until the loopback shows the instance in neither service; a reopened loopback watch completes pending departures (`5d3ff4d`) |

While checking, the implementation found a third defect itself: a watch asked for right after the cell's first record appeared, but before the hub learned that its write was stored, was refused (2 of 30 runs). A watch now waits for the epoch to be settled, in process as long as its caller does and on the route up to 5 s, then 503 (`1d8e121`).
