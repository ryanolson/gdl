1. **P2 — Remove timer registration from the per-item path.** [crates/gdl/src/dist/hop.rs:660](/home/ryan/repos/dynamo-gdl/crates/gdl/src/dist/hop.rs:660). Each pump iteration creates and polls `cx.done()` before reading an item, then drops it. Tokio 1.53.3 registers and removes that deadline timer under its shared timer-driver mutex: approximately 2,000 shared-lock acquisitions per 1,000-item producing run, even with ready input and available output capacity. Concurrent streams therefore contend on a runtime-wide lock, contrary to the hot-path contract. **Change wanted:** pin the interruption future outside the loop and reuse it, preserving interruption handling during blocked sends; measure with concurrent streams.

2. **P2 — Separate allocator instrumentation from timing measurements.** [crates/gdl/benches/remote.rs:70](/home/ryan/repos/dynamo-gdl/crates/gdl/benches/remote.rs:70). `Counting` remains enabled throughout Criterion and implements only `alloc` and `dealloc`. Consequently, `realloc` uses the trait’s allocate-copy-free fallback instead of `System.realloc`, changing how MessagePack buffers grow. Every allocation/deallocation also updates shared atomic counters. Gdl’s additional serialization work incurs more of this instrumentation, so the documented codec gap cannot be attributed solely to production encoding and copying costs. **Change wanted:** forward allocator operations faithfully for allocation reporting, run latency/throughput/codec measurements with instrumentation compiled out, and update `m3-remote.md` from those results.
