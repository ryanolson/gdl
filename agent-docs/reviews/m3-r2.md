All five were valid. Each code finding landed with a test that failed before its fix. Ruling 17 has a dated addendum.

| # | Severity | Finding | Ruling | Evidence and fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1 | With the response held unread, a cancel or stop of the caller's context never reached the producer: the watcher exited on cancel without touching the anchor, and stop was forwarded only when the stream was read | valid | `a_cancel_reaches_the_remote_sink_while_the_stream_is_held_unread` and `a_stop_reaches_the_remote_sink_while_the_stream_is_held_unread` both timed out before the fix. Fix: the watcher forwards stop as it arrives and retires the anchor on cancel |
| 2 | P1 | At the deadline, cancelling velo's controller left frames already in the anchor's receive buffer until the stream was read or dropped | valid | velo's `StreamAnchor` owns the mux feed's receiver (`streaming/anchor.rs:571-600`); `StreamController::cancel` only removes the registry entry and cancels the sender. `a_held_stream_delivers_nothing_buffered_after_its_deadline`: before the fix, a held stream read after its deadline returned `Truncated`. Fix: `fabric::Inbound::share` puts the anchor behind a slot the watcher can empty. At the deadline or cancel the watcher drops the anchor, which releases its buffer and cancels the producer, and the held stream's next read is the reason. Measured: a held stream past its deadline keeps 0.8 KiB and no tasks (`agent-docs/perf/m3-remote.md`). The lock costs one uncontended acquire per remote item |
| 3 | P1 | Setup work in another segment was bounded only by the setup timeout, not by the request: a delayed acknowledgement held the hand-off task and its admission share past the deadline | valid | `a_delayed_hand_off_ack_does_not_hold_remote_work_past_the_deadline`: with a 4 s delayed ack and a 300 ms deadline, the side segment held the request after 1.5 s. Fix: the onward hand-off, attaches, the egress wait in `open_run`, and failure delivery are all bounded by the request as well as the setup timeout |
| 4 | P2 | A zero attempt limit left one ledger entry per refused root | valid | `a_zero_limit_is_refused_at_deployment`. Fix: zero admission, attempt, and egress limits are refused by `Deployment::new` |
| 5 | P2 | The perf attribution compared gdl's ticket open with velo's plain attach | valid | The direct baseline now uses the ticket path for every metric, and plain attach is reported alongside. Correction: the 20 us per-item gap at 16 KiB in the earlier version was attach against ticket, not gdl. Matched, gdl is at the direct latency, with 2 more spawned tasks and 48 more allocations per request, and 2 to 9% lower item throughput |
