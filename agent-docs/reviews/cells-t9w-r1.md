# Error kinds and watch events on the wire: review round 1

Reviewer: an independent reviewer, read-only, with the `thermo-nuclear-code-quality-review` and `rust-code-review` skills, in `/tmp` copies with their own target directories (2026-10-23). Target: `ce341e8..d8c5191`, the owner's ruling of 2026-10-23 on how new kinds cross the wire, which settles what `cells-t9-r1.md` left pending:

- **Error kinds are tolerant and native** (`d7b6fde`, `edf0915`). `OutcomeUnknown` is sent as its own kind, and the `[outcome unknown] ` / `[transport] ` message prefixes are gone. A kind this build does not know is read as `Transport` and keeps its name: `Display` shows it and `Error::unknown_kind()` returns it. msgpack sends a kind by its name, so every earlier kind's bytes are unchanged; an index is read too. This one change rolls in lockstep.
- **An unknown watch event fails closed for its cell** (`0f0fead`, `0138b4a`, `80543a1`). Every event names its cell, and an event of a kind the reader does not know withholds that cell, and only that cell, until it is read again. Later information goes in as optional fields on known events. The owner replaced "skip and continue" with this rule before anything was committed.

Verdict: REJECT, three major findings, all in how a withheld cell was read again. The error-kind part passed its checks.

Ruling by the coordinator on a question the fix raised: a follow link that meets an unknown event replaces its connection to the cell's hub, keeping the follow, its relay entry, and its generation, with no `Dropped`. The owner's rule protects two things: the cell does not flap, and no other cell is affected. A follow link's watch is of one cell, so this keeps both. A sequence number in the event stream's `id:` line would keep the connection, but it is a wire change the rule does not need.
