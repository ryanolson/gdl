Reviewed `0035d75`. The home/near cap and RTT revisions resolve their round-four findings. The following issues remain.

1. **Major — Temporary unfollow uses permanent retirement promises (§12.2, [cells.md:455](agent-docs/cells.md:455)).** `gdl-avoids` promises never to route to that instance again; retirement and acknowledgement marks survive subsequent joins. Refollowing a healthy cell with unchanged instance IDs therefore cannot restore ordinary routing. Retiring every entry also invokes the existing fallback to unacknowledged retirees, which prevents the expected acknowledgements. Evidence: [roster.rs:127](crates/gdl/src/dist/roster.rs:127), [roster.rs:159](crates/gdl/src/dist/roster.rs:159), [roster.rs:387](crates/gdl/src/dist/roster.rs:387). **Change:** introduce reversible, follow-generation-scoped eligibility and “no new offers” acknowledgements, separate from actual instance retirement. Fence delayed unregisters across refollow. Test unfollow and refollow with unchanged entry instances.

2. **Major — Policy unfollow can truncate healthy accepted requests (§12.2, [cells.md:462](agent-docs/cells.md:462)).** `drain_ms` bounds a *stopped* instance; it can be shorter than an admitted request’s deadline. An RTT or cap eviction does not stop the model cell, yet the proposed timer unregisters its peers and fails outstanding work. A healthy twenty-second stream can therefore be cut by a one-second drain setting. Evidence: [runtime.rs:56](crates/gdl/src/dist/runtime.rs:56), [fabric.rs:74](crates/gdl/src/dist/fabric.rs:74). **Change:** separate routing eligibility from transport lifetime; retain registrations until admitted work retires or reaches its deadlines. Test a long stream across policy unfollow with a shorter configured drain bound.

3. **Major — Reciprocal introductions do not maintain a full mesh (§12.3, [cells.md:474](agent-docs/cells.md:474)).** Suppose B and C, in different regions, read seed A’s peer list before either reports. Both introduce themselves to A, and A follows both back, but neither learns the other. Renewing their reports does not reveal third-party membership, and the one-hop export rule explicitly prevents A from forwarding model records learned through B to C ([cells.md:475](agent-docs/cells.md:475)). The new seed-turnover test can pass while this join race remains. **Change:** watch peer membership or periodically reconcile peer lists, introducing to seeds themselves as well as discovered peers. Gate both initial reads before either introduction; require B↔C discovery and model visibility after A leaves.

4. **Major — Authentication at `_hello` does not cover velo’s connection lifecycle (§13 V1/Q11, [cells.md:537](agent-docs/cells.md:537)).** QUIC dispatches decoded frames without requiring `_hello`; the normal hello preparation is enforced by the sender, and system handlers bypass it. Legitimate reverse connections can also begin with a response rather than a hello. Checking only that handler leaves bypasses; requiring hello as every connection’s first message breaks existing paths. Evidence: [listener.rs:133](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo/src/transports/quic/listener.rs:133), [client/mod.rs:388](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo/src/messenger/client/mod.rs:388), [handlers/mod.rs:1066](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo/src/messenger/handlers/mod.rs:1066). **Change:** require authenticated identity before dispatch on every connection and lane, including reverse traffic. Velo owns that mechanism; gdl supplies cell authorization. Add §14 rejection tests for direct traffic without authentication, alongside response-first and mux-first positive controls.

5. **Major — Q11(a) lacks an enrollment authority (§13, [cells.md:537](agent-docs/cells.md:537)).** Membership in a trusted hub does not currently prove authorization. A new owner can register without presenting a credential, and gdl admission checks public deployment identity rather than caller identity. An unauthorized process can therefore enroll its own fingerprint and satisfy the proposed membership check. HTTPS alone does not close this gap. Evidence: [authority.rs:400](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo-hub/src/hub/authority.rs:400), [gdl-hub/lib.rs:41](crates/gdl-hub/src/lib.rs:41). **Change:** specify authenticated initial enrollment and independently trusted membership/fingerprint provenance for option (a). This policy belongs in gdl’s admission/deployment layer. Add tests proving that unauthorized enrollment and hub reports cannot change trusted state.

6. **Major — Hub-restart survival has no compatible recovery lifecycle (§13, [cells.md:539](agent-docs/cells.md:539)).** The new requirement conflicts with §5’s lease-loss termination rule. Re-registration alone also cannot preserve current routing: the restarted admission extension initially has an empty membership map; surviving followers can interpret incomplete recovery as departures and permanently mark healthy instances left. Evidence: [cells.md:271](agent-docs/cells.md:271), [gdl-hub/lib.rs:18](crates/gdl-hub/src/lib.rs:18), [fabric/hub.rs:627](crates/gdl/src/dist/fabric/hub.rs:627), [roster.rs:142](crates/gdl/src/dist/roster.rs:142). **Change:** either defer survival explicitly and retain fail-stop behavior, or define recovery gating, lifecycle-state restoration, and reconciliation that preserves genuine retirement promises without treating incomplete recovery as departure. Test staggered re-registration of surviving instance IDs.

7. **Minor — The velo dependency inventory overstates missing capabilities (§13 V2, V3, V5).** HTTPS clients already support a configured TLS client ([client/mod.rs:183](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo-hub/src/client/mod.rs:183)). `InterfaceFilter::Explicit` already overrides advertised IPs, though it overwrites their ports ([interfaces.rs:210](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo/src/transports/utils/interfaces.rs:210)). `Velo::refresh_handlers` already forces hello and warms ordinary lane-zero connections ([lib.rs:1154](/home/ryan/repos/velo/lib/velo/src/lib.rs:1154)). **Change:** scope V2 to server TLS or a proxy, V3 to missing address/port remapping, and V5 to any richer connection-warming requirement that existing APIs cannot meet. Update §14’s prerequisites accordingly.

**Verdict: REJECT.**
