**Major — §12.2 can reuse an acknowledgement from an earlier exclusion.** Target: `agent-docs/cells.md:456–457`.

The acknowledgement identifies `(M, follow generation g)`, but eligibility can change repeatedly without changing g:

1. G excludes M; instance P publishes `gdl-unoffered-M-g`.
2. G re-enables M before closing the follow; delivery to P and withdrawal of P’s acknowledgement are delayed.
3. G excludes M again. P’s first acknowledgement remains visible and can satisfy this second exclusion.
4. G closes g. P then processes the queued re-enable and briefly offers new work before receiving the second exclusion/drop.

Other instances can acknowledge the second exclusion while P’s stale acknowledgement satisfies its part. The per-offer lease does not protect a request that starts after the hub mistakenly concluded that exclusion was complete.

**Change:** give each eligibility transition a revision and acknowledge `(M, g, exclusion revision)`. An acknowledgement for an earlier exclusion must never close a later one. Alternatively, require a complete withdrawal barrier before re-enabling, but that changes the stated immediate reversibility.

Test a delayed re-enable delivery followed by another exclusion; retain the old acknowledgement and verify that it cannot authorize closure.
